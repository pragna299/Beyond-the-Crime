Why Do People Become Criminals?

When a robbery, cyberattack, or violent crime appears in the news, most people focus on what happened. Very few stop to ask a deeper question: Why?

What transforms an ordinary person into a criminal? Is it poverty? Trauma? Environment? Choice? Or something hidden deep within the human mind?

Beyond the Crime investigates these questions through psychology, behavioral science, real-world cases, and social research. Our goal is not only to understand criminal behavior, but also to explore how individuals and societies can create positive change.
